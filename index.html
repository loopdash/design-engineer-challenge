Design Engineer challenge

Arkhelm Aerospace is a made-up rocket company. It needs an Open House RSVP page. Design it, build it, and ship it as a pull request in about 3 hours.

The task

Dana runs communications at Arkhelm. Her email has the request and links to the current site, the brand sheet, and the event facts.

  1. Build the Open House page at site/open-house.html. Make it look like part of the homepage. A visitor should see what, when, and where right away. The RSVP form needs hover, focus, invalid, submitting, and success states on phone and desktop. There's no backend, so fake the submit.
  2. Make Dana's three homepage changes. Some of them work against good design. Decide, and say why.
  3. Turn the brand into a few CSS variables. Use them on both pages.
  4. Reply to Dana. Write the email you'd send her when you finish and commit it as CLIENT_EMAIL.md. Tell her what's ready, what isn't, and anything you need from her. Keep it short enough to read on a phone.

Arkhelm, Dana, and everyone else here are made up. Don't look anyone up or contact anyone.

Use AI

We use Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Figma AI, and MCP tools every day. Use them here too. In your pull request, paste one prompt that worked and one thing the AI got wrong that you fixed.

Submit

  1. Copy the repo from our template and set it to Private, then work on a branch. Use plain HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript, with no frameworks or build step.
  2. Open a pull request to main in your copy. The template asks for screenshots, what you did and skipped, your AI example, and 3 UX ideas beyond the brief.
  3. Add the reviewer named in your invite as a collaborator on your repo.
  4. Email us the PR link at the address in your invite.

When time's up, stop and list what's left in the PR. We'd rather see one finished page than a rushed pass at every task.

After you submit

We'll set up a 30 minute call to walk through your page. You don't need to prepare.

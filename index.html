Loopdash take-home

Design Engineer challenge

Loopdash is a small web agency. In this role you read client email, design and build pages from a client's brand standards, ship them as pull requests, and explain what changed in plain language. This exercise has you do each of those once.

The scenario

Dana, head of communications at Arkhelm Aerospace (a made-up rocket startup), has emailed asking for a new Open House RSVP page and a few quick homepage changes. She attached the current site, the brand sheet, and the event notes.

Arkhelm, Dana, Marcus, and everything else here are fictional. Do not look anyone up or contact anyone.

What to deliver

  1. A short plan: what you'll do, in what order, and what you'll skip.
  2. The Open House page, designed and built, with a working RSVP form and all its states.
  3. Dana's three quick homepage asks, using your judgment where they pull against good design.
  4. A small set of design tokens as CSS custom properties, used on both pages.
  5. A pull request with screenshots, what changed, what you skipped, and how you tested.
  6. One example of how you used AI, and one thing you corrected.
  7. A plain-language wrap-up email to Dana.
  8. Your 3 best UX improvements beyond what Dana asked for.

The full details for each item are in the README.

Time and tools

Time box
About 3 hours, 4 at most. Spend most of it on the new page. When time is up, stop and list what's left.
Tools
Anything you like: Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, MCP tools, Figma. Tell us what you used and where it helped.

Ground rules

  • Plain HTML and CSS, plus a little vanilla JavaScript for the form. No frameworks or build tools.
  • Use the brand sheet's logo, colors, fonts, and photos. Don't add new brand colors or fonts.
  • Leave the root index.html and email/index.html alone. They are the brief, not the site.

How to submit

  1. Fork the repo and make a branch, such as yourname/open-house.
  2. Commit as you go with clear messages.
  3. Open a pull request to this repo's main branch and fill in the template.
  4. Send us the PR link at the email address in your invite.

What happens next

We'll set up a 30 minute call. You'll walk through your page and PR and explain your choices. For a few minutes we'll play Dana and bring you a new request, and you'll answer as you would on a real client call. Nothing to prepare beyond your submission.

Hi,

Marcus from our board passed along your name. I run the Pellbrook Garden Alliance. We run the community gardens here in Pellbrook. A volunteer built our website a few years ago and then moved away, and nobody has really touched it since. Sorry if this is a bit of a dump. I am writing from my phone in the garden shed because we do not have wifi out here.

The short version is we want more people to show up and more people to give. Saturday volunteer days have been thin this year and donations are down, and we think the site is part of that. Most people find us from the QR code on the garden gates or the link in our newsletter, so they are almost always on their phones. On my phone the buttons pile up on top of each other and the words run off the side of the screen.

I went through the site with the board on Tuesday. There were a lot of opinions. Here is what I wrote down:

  • It needs to pop more. Right now it feels flat and everything kind of falls in line with everything else. Nothing stands out.
  • The logo should be bigger. Marcus says exactly 20% bigger, so let's go with that.
  • The Get Involved button is lost in with all the other buttons. It should stand out more, maybe with a picture. Marcus wants a giant Get Involved button. His words.
  • We need a donate button people actually see. Right now the only way to give is a little link at the very bottom. But when we tried a big one on the old site it looked retail, like we were selling something. Maybe smaller but brighter? Something in between. Whatever it looks like, it should say Give Today.
  • People should see everything we do without scrolling. The gardens, the youth program, the tool library, the seedling sale, the events. Can it all fit in the first screen or two?
  • The green is off. My husband is a graphic designer and he says everything has a washed-out pale green over it, when our logo is a deep green. Can it match our t-shirts? I can mail you one.
  • The photo at the top must have been taken on a cloudy day. Can you make it sunny?
  • The Contact link just jumps down the page. Nobody would know to do that.
  • Ways To Help is buried at the bottom. Can it go right under the numbers, above Our Mission?
  • The tool library hours are out of date. It is open Saturdays 9am to 1pm now, not 9 to noon.
  • Volunteer sign up should be on there somehow. Not sure if that is a form or a link to the Google Form we already use. You tell us.
  • The numbers at the top have placeholders in them. I will get you the real numbers. Leave those for now.

Also, and I know this is beyond the homepage, but while you are in there: we want to start selling our seedlings online this year instead of only at the May sale. Could you add a little store where people pick what they want and pay? We would need it live by the end of next week so we can announce it in the April newsletter. Marcus says we can use his Square account.

The site files are attached (our volunteer kept them in a shared folder). Please do not turn it into anything fancy. I update the text myself by opening the file, and I am not technical. Our donation page is at donate.example.org/pellbrook-garden-alliance if that helps.

What do you think, and what do you need from me?

Design Engineer challenge

Arkhelm Aerospace, a made-up rocket company, needs an Open House RSVP page. Design it, build it, and ship it as a pull request. About 3 hours.

The task

Dana, Arkhelm's head of communications, has emailed with the request. Her email links to everything you need: the current site, the brand sheet, and the event facts.

  1. Build the Open House page at site/open-house.html. It should feel like the same site as the homepage, tell visitors what, when, and where in seconds, and have an RSVP form that handles every state (hover, focus, invalid, submitting, success) on phone and desktop. There's no backend, so fake the submit.
  2. Handle Dana's three quick homepage asks. Some pull against good design. Make a call and say why.
  3. Pull the brand into a few CSS variables and use them on both pages.
  4. Reply to Dana. Write the email you'd send her when you're done, in CLIENT_EMAIL.md. Plain language she can read on her phone: what's ready, what isn't, and what you need from her.

Arkhelm, Dana, and everyone else here are fictional. Don't look anyone up or contact anyone.

Use AI

We work with Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Figma AI, and MCP tools every day, and we want to see how you work with them too. Use whatever gets you to a better page faster. In your pull request, share one prompt that worked well and one thing the AI got wrong that you fixed.

Submit

  1. Fork the repo and work on a branch. Plain HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript. No frameworks or build step.
  2. Open a pull request to main. The template asks for screenshots, what you did and skipped, your AI example, and 3 UX ideas beyond the brief.
  3. Email us the PR link at the address in your invite.

When time's up, stop and list what's left in the PR. A polished page with a short to-do list beats a rushed pass at everything.

After you submit

We'll set up a 30 minute call to walk through your page and choices. For a few minutes we'll play Dana with a new request. Nothing to prepare.

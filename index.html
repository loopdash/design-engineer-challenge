Welcome To The Pellbrook Garden Alliance Website

Growing Community, One Plot At A Time, In Pellbrook, Ohio, Since 2011

We are a 501(c)(3) non profit organization that manages 6 community gardens accross Pellbrook, Ohio and we run a youth gardening program and a seedling sale and also we have a tool library and we have been doing this since 2011 and we are always looking for more people to get involved so please get involved.

The Pellbrook Garden Alliance turns empty lots into growing spaces. Anyone in Pellbrook can rent a plot for the season, learn to grow food, and share the harvest. Plot fees are kept low and nobody is turned away for inability to pay.

Our Gardens

Maple Street Garden

Our first garden. 42 plots, a tool shed, and a shared herb bed. Rain barrels on every corner.

Riverside Lot

Opened in 2019 on the old laundromat site. 28 raised beds, all wheelchair accessible.

Eastside School Garden

Run with the 4th grade classes at Eastside Elementary. Kids plant in April and harvest in September.

Programs

Youth Growers

A summer program for kids ages 8 to 14. Every Tuesday and Thursday in July. Kids get their own bed, learn the basics, and take home what they grow. Free, lunch included.

Tool Library

Members can borrow shovels, hoses, wheelbarrows, and a tiller. Open Saturdays 9 to noon at the Maple Street shed.

Ways To Help

There are lots of ways to help out! You can volunteer on Saturdays, you can rent a plot, you can donate tools, or you can make a financial contribution. If you would like to make a financial contribution please contact our treasurer and she will tell you how to do that. We accept checks.

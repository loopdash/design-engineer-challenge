Our mission

The Pellbrook Garden Alliance turns empty lots into growing spaces. Anyone in Pellbrook can rent a plot for the season, learn to grow food, and share the harvest.

Plot fees are kept low and nobody is turned away for inability to pay. Each fall, gardeners accross all six sites donate their extra produce to the Pellbrook Community Pantry.

What we do

Youth Growers

A free summer program for kids ages 8 to 14, every Tuesday and Thursday in July. Kids get their own bed and take home what they grow.

Tool Library

Members can borrow shovels, hoses, wheelbarrows, and a tiller. Open Saturdays 9 to noon at the Maple Street shed.

Seedling Sale

Every May we sell tomato, pepper, and herb seedlings from our greenhouse. Proceeds pay for plot subsidies.

Get involved

Volunteer days are every Saturday morning from April through October. No experience needed, and we provide gloves and tools. You can also rent a plot, donate tools, or help at the May seedling sale.

To make a financial gift, please contact our treasurer at hello@pellbrookgardens.example.org.

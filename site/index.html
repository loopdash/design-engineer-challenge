Systems

One vehicle. Three things we build ourselves.

Kite-1

A two-stage launch vehicle that puts up to 600kg into low Earth orbit. The first stage flies back and lands near the pad.

SpurEngine

Spur engine

A 3D-printed liquid engine. Nine power the first stage. We hot-fire every engine twice before it flies.

Lattice flight software

Guidance, navigation, and landing software written in-house and flown on every mission since Flight 3.

Test Range

Sable Flats

We build, test, and launch from 40 square miles of high desert. The factory sits three miles from the pad, so hardware goes from the floor to a hot-fire in an afternoon.

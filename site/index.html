The Pellbrook Garden Alliance turns empty lots into growing spaces. Anyone in Pellbrook can rent a plot for the season, learn to grow food, and share the harvest. Plot fees are kept low and nobody is turned away for inability to pay.

What We Do

Youth Growers

A free summer program for kids ages 8 to 14. Every Tuesday and Thursday in July. Kids get their own bed and take home what they grow.

Tool Library

Members can borrow shovels, hoses, wheelbarrows, and a tiller. Open Saturdays 9 to noon at the Maple Street shed.

Seedling Sale

Every May we sell tomato, pepper, and herb seedlings from our greenhouse. Proceeds pay for plot subsidies.

Ways To Help

There are lots of ways to help out! You can volunteer on Saturdays, you can rent a plot, you can donate tools, or you can make a financial contribution. If you would like to make a financial contribution please contact our treasurer and she will tell you how to do that. We accept checks.

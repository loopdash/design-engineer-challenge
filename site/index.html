What We Do

Smart Scheduling

Patients book online 24/7. Your calendar fills gaps on its own and keeps each therapist's hours in one place.

Automated Reminders

Text and email reminders go out two days and two hours before every visit. Patients confirm or reschedule with one tap.

Insurance Billing

Claims are built from the visit notes and sent the same day. Denials come back to one queue so nothing gets lost.

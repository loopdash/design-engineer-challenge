04 Photography

Real hardware, big and quiet

Full bleed, high contrast, lots of sky or shadow. Show the work: vehicles, engines, the range, people building. No stock handshakes, no lens flares added in post, no other company's markings.

Put text over photos only with a Carbon scrim at 50% or more. Credit every photo.

05 Voice

How we sound

Short, declarative, specific. We let the hardware do the bragging.

  • Say what it does. Then stop.One idea per sentence. Cut the warm-up.
  • Numbers beat adjectives."14 flights" says more than "proven."
  • No hype words.Not revolutionary, disruptive, game-changing, or world-class.
  • Write like an engineer briefing a smart friend.Plain words. Explain the term or drop it.
  • Active voice, present tense."We test every engine twice," not "Engines are tested."
  • Credit the team, not the company.People built it. Say who.
